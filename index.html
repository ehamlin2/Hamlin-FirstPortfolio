Behind the Camera

Hi, my name is Elisabeth, and I am a photographer focused on creating natural, meaningful images that feel true to the people and moments in them.

My work centers around family, lifestyle, and portrait photography, with experience photographing families, maternity sessions, graduations, headshots, pets, rescue animals, wildlife, events, and other creative projects.

My photography style is rooted in storytelling. I love images with natural emotion, strong composition, true-to-life skin tones, and rich color, and I aim for photographs that tell a story rather than simply document a pose. The result is images that feel warm, expressive, and personal while still having a polished, artistic finish.

Featured Work

People

Family photography is at the heart of much of my work. I enjoy capturing the connection between people in a way that feels natural, warm, and genuine.

My family work includes traditional family portraits, maternity sessions, professional headshots, and everyday life. I aim for a balance of lightly guided portraits and candid moments so the final gallery feels polished without losing personality.

Experience

I have experience photographing families, children, portraits, maternity sessions, graduations, headshots, animals, community events, and creative projects.

My work has included photographing clients and events throughout South Carolina, as well as projects connected with local organizations and community programs.

In addition to photographing sessions, I handle the full creative process, including planning, location selection, lighting, image culling, color correction, retouching, and final delivery.

Editing & Equipment

I work with both natural light and off-camera lighting depending on the session and environment.

My editing workflow includes Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop, with a focus on maintaining realistic skin tones while creating depth, color, and a finished look that still feels natural.

I also enjoy experimenting with different cameras, lenses, lighting setups, and editing techniques to continue developing my work.

Education & Continuing Development

Alongside my photography work, I am currently completing my undergraduate education through Arizona State University.

My coursework in web development and digital technology has also allowed me to develop skills in HTML, CSS, responsive design, and digital presentation. I enjoy being able to use those skills to build and manage the online side of my creative work.
